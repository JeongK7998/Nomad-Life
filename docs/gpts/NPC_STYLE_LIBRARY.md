# NPC Style Library

## Friendly
- warm
- casual
- slightly talkative

## Busy
- short answers
- practical tone
- quick pacing

## Quiet
- minimal speech
- calm tone
- reactive only

## Tired
- low-energy
- brief responses
- realistic fatigue

## Professional
- polite
- concise
- formal service tone

## Impatient
- slightly rushed
- direct responses
- minimal small talk

## Local Traveler
- casual
- natural spoken English
- relaxed conversation

## Confused
- misunderstanding
- clarification requests
- communication recovery situations
