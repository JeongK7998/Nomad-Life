# English Roleplay Review — Store / Sleeveless Shirt Shopping

- **Date:** 2026-05-22
- **Scenario:** Local clothing store
- **User role:** Customer
- **Assistant role:** Store clerk
- **Main goal:** Buy sleeveless shirts and casual shorts

---

## 1. Conversation Summary

You entered a local clothing store and tried to buy clothes for a hot day. The main target item was a **sleeveless shirt / tank top**, but there were several communication breakdowns before the meaning became clear. After that, you asked about size, color, promotions, shorts, fitting room, payment, receipt, and a bag.

You successfully completed the shopping journey from product search to checkout.

---

## 2. Strong Points

### Good survival ability
You kept speaking even when the clerk misunderstood you.

Examples:
- “No, no, no.”
- “Yes, that’s right.”
- “Can I try it?”
- “I’ll pay by card.”

### Good shopping flow vocabulary
You used many useful real-life shopping expressions:

- try it
- bigger one
- different color
- promotion
- pay by card
- receipt
- free

### Good recovery behavior
Even after misunderstanding “sleeveless,” you continued until the meaning was clear. This is very important for real conversations.

---

## 3. Main Weak Points

### 1) Key pronunciation / word choice issue
You wanted to say **sleeveless**, but it sounded like:

- slavery
- sleepless
- something unclear

Correct expression:

> Do you have sleeveless shirts?

Natural alternatives:

> Do you have tank tops?

> I’m looking for a sleeveless shirt.

---

### 2) Korean sentence order influence
Some sentences followed Korean structure too closely.

Original:

> What is the best of the one?

More natural:

> Which one do you recommend?

> Which one is best for hot weather?

---

### 3) Unclear object reference
Sometimes “this,” “one,” or “it” was unclear.

Original:

> Do you have anything to deliver this?

Possible intended meaning:

> Do you have this in sleeveless?

or

> Do you have this in another style?

---

### 4) Price / size confusion
Original:

> My salary is $30 per Korean.

Possible intended meaning:

> My budget is around $30.

or

> My waist size is 30 in Korean size.

Natural shopping phrase:

> My budget is around $30.

> I usually wear size 30.

---

## 4. Useful Corrected Phrases

| Situation | Natural English |
|---|---|
| Looking for sleeveless shirt | I’m looking for a sleeveless shirt. |
| Easier casual version | Do you have tank tops? |
| Asking recommendation | Which one do you recommend? |
| Asking for size | Do you have a bigger size? |
| Asking for color | Does this come in another color? |
| Asking promotion | Do you have any promotions? |
| Trying clothes | Can I try this on? |
| Buying two items | I’ll take these two. |
| Asking total price | How much is it in total? |
| Asking for bag | Do you have a paper bag? |
| Asking if free | Is the bag free? |
