# Rental Car Airport Roleplay Review

## 1. Session Summary

**Scenario:** Airport rental car counter  
**User Role:** Customer  
**Assistant Role:** Rental car staff  
**Main Goal:** Pick up a reserved compact car, upgrade to a larger automatic SUV, arrange one-way return, add full insurance, check the car condition, and ask about accessories.

You handled the situation well. You kept the conversation moving, explained your needs, asked about price, insurance, deposit, documents, and car condition. The main weak point was not communication failure, but sentence structure and natural phrasing.

## 3. Main Weak Points

## Issue 1 — “I can driving” structure

### Your sentence
> I can only driving automatic cars.  
> I can't driving manual car.

### Natural version
> I can only drive automatic cars.  
> I can’t drive a manual car.

### Pattern
After **can / can’t**, use the base verb.

- can drive
- can return
- can change
- can pay
- can upgrade

### Practice
- I can only drive automatic cars.
- I can’t drive manual cars.
- Can I return it to another location?

---

## Issue 2 — “more bigger”

### Your sentence
> I want to change the more bigger than compact car.

### Natural version
> I’d like to upgrade to a bigger car.

### Pattern
Do not use **more + bigger** together.

Use:

- bigger
- larger
- more spacious

### Practice
- I’d like a bigger car.
- Do you have a larger vehicle?
- I need something more spacious.

---

## Issue 3 — “reservation about a car”

### Your sentence
> I made a reservation about a car.

### Natural version
> I made a reservation for a car.

### Pattern
Use **reservation for + item/service**.

### Practice
- I have a reservation for a car.
- I made a reservation for a compact car.
- I booked a compact car online.

---

## Issue 4 — Explaining luggage and passengers

### Your sentence
> I have a lot of extra companions and extra baggage.

### Natural version
> I’m traveling with several people and a lot of luggage.

### Better airport rental phrase
> We have three large suitcases, so we need a car with a big trunk.

### Practice
- We have a lot of luggage.
- We have three large suitcases.
- We need a car with enough trunk space.

---

## Issue 5 — “cost really press me”

### Your sentence
> It’s cost really press me.

### Natural version
> The cost is a bit high for me.  
> I’m worried about the total cost.

### Practice
- I’m worried about the price.
- Is there a cheaper option?
- Do you have any discounts?
- Is there a promotion for six days?

---

## Issue 6 — One-way return phrasing

### Your sentence
> Can I return to another station?

### Natural version
> Can I return the car to another location?

### Better version
> Can I drop it off at another branch?

### Practice
- Can I return the car to another location?
- Can I drop it off at the south branch?
- Is there a one-way rental fee?

---

## Issue 7 — Insurance phrase

### Your sentence
> I need a total package insurance.

### Natural version
> I’d like full insurance coverage.

### Practice
- I’d like full coverage.
- What does the full insurance include?
- Does it cover damage and theft?

---

## Issue 8 — Damage check phrase

### Your sentence
> Can I check the extension, if it’s carrier about some damage?

### Natural version
> Can we check the car for any existing damage?

### Better version
> Can we do a quick walk-around before I leave?

### Practice
- Can we check for scratches or dents?
- Can you note this damage on the form?
- I want to make sure it’s recorded.

---

## 4. Strong Useful Phrases for Next Time

### At the counter
- Hi, I have a reservation for a rental car.
- My reservation number is here.
- I booked a compact car, but I’d like to upgrade.

### Car type
- Do you have an automatic SUV available?
- We have three large suitcases.
- We need enough trunk space.

### Price
- How much is the total for six days?
- Is there any promotion available?
- Is the upgrade fee included?

### Return location
- Can I return the car to another location?
- Can I drop it off at the south branch?
- Is there a one-way fee?

### Insurance
- I’d like full insurance coverage.
- What does it include?
- Does it cover scratches, dents, and theft?

### Documents and payment
- Here is my credit card.
- Here is my driver’s license.
- Do you also need my passport?

### Before leaving
- Can we check the car for existing damage?
- Can we do a quick walk-around?
- Could you note this scratch on the form?

---

## 5. Recommended Mini Drill

Practice this full version aloud:

> Hi, I have a reservation for a compact car.  
> But I’m traveling with several people and three large suitcases, so I’d like to upgrade to a bigger automatic SUV.  
> I’m worried about the price, so do you have any promotions?  
> Also, can I return the car to another location?  
> I’d like full insurance coverage, and before I leave, can we check the car for any existing damage?
