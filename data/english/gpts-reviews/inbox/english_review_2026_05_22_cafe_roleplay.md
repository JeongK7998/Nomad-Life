## 5. Main Weak Points

### 5.1 Pronunciation / recognition confusion

Some phrases were difficult to understand:

| Spoken Attempt | Likely Intended Meaning | Natural Version |
|---|---|---|
| “hover copy past” | hot coffee, perhaps black | “a hot black coffee” |
| “just of flex” | just black | “just black, please” |
| “cloud shade and popcan shade” | crunchy seeds / pumpkin seeds | “some crunchy seeds, like pumpkin seeds” |
| “mixed pit notch” | mixed peanuts / mixed nuts | “mixed nuts” |
| “frappe” | refill / another beer? | “Can I get a refill?” / “Can I get another beer?” |
| “I wanted to beer” | I want the bill | “I wanted the bill.” |

### 5.2 Korean sentence order influence

Example:

> “Can I move the table on the upstairs?”

Natural:

> “Can I move to a table upstairs?”

The meaning was understandable, but the object order sounded Korean-influenced.

### 5.3 Asking about ingredients

Example:

> “What made it?”

Natural options:

- “What is it made of?”
- “What’s in it?”
- “What ingredients are in it?”

For casual situations, **“What’s in it?”** is the most natural.

---

## 6. Useful Replacement Phrases

### Ordering coffee

- “Can I get a hot black coffee?”
- “Just black, please.”
- “No sugar or honey, please.”

### Asking about food

- “What’s in the fruit bowl?”
- “Does it come with anything sweet?”
- “Can you add some pumpkin seeds?”
- “Do you have any bread that isn’t toasted?”

### Handling a wrong order

- “Excuse me, I think this isn’t my order.”
- “I ordered the fruit bowl, but this looks like yogurt.”
- “How long will it take?”
- “My coffee is getting cold.”

### Beer / snack order

- “Can I get a local craft beer?”
- “What’s the lightest local beer you have?”
- “Do you have any snacks that go well with beer?”
- “Mixed nuts would be great.”

### Payment

- “Can I get the bill?”
- “How much is it in total?”
- “Can I pay by card?”
