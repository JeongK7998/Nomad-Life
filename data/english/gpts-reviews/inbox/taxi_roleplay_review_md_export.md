# Main Corrections & Natural Alternatives

## 1. “drop off her”

### Your sentence
> “Can you drop off her to the pink mart?”

### More natural
- “Can you drop her off at the mart?”
- “Can you drop my wife off at the mart first?”

### Important Pattern
“drop off + person + at + place”

### Examples
- “Drop me off at the hotel.”
- “Drop him off at the station.”

---

## 2. “highway pair / highway P”

You were trying to say:
- highway fare
- highway toll
- toll fee

### Natural versions
- “How much is the highway toll?”
- “Is there a toll fee?”
- “Do we need to pay for the highway?”

This was actually a very realistic communication moment.
Even native speakers ask again when pronunciation is unclear.

---

## 3. “can you fast get that possible?”

Your meaning was clear.

### More natural
- “Can you get there quickly?”
- “Can we make it in time?”
- “Could you drive a little faster?”

---

## 4. “I’m going to check in about the American Express”

This sentence became slightly unclear.

You probably meant:
- airline lounge
- Amex counter
- check-in desk

### Natural alternatives
- “Do you know where the Amex check-in counter is?”
- “Can you drop me near the Amex lounge entrance?”
- “Which terminal is the Amex counter in?”
