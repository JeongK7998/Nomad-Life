# Hotel Check-in Session Review

## A. Key Expressions

- “Do you have a reservation?”
- “Here’s my passport.”
- “I’d like a room on a higher floor.”
- “Can I check in early?”
- “Is breakfast included?”
- “Where is the dining area?”
- “What time is check-out?”
- “Can I use the pool or gym?”
- “I’d like an extra key.”

---

## B. Corrected Expressions

- “and like higher profits” → “I’d like a higher floor.”

- “Can I get in early?” → “Can I check in early?”

- “Yeah, and is it included the breakfast?” → “Is breakfast included?”

- “I’d like to gather extra key” → “I’d like an extra key.”

- “because my wife look around separately with me” → “Because my wife and I may go separately.”

---

## C. Critical Grammar Issues

- Missing articles:

  - “extra key” → “an extra key”

- Wrong verb choice:

  - “gather extra key” sounds unnatural
  - Better:
    - “get”
    - “have”
    - “request”

- Word confusion:

  - “profits” instead of “floor”

---

## D. Pronunciation Risk

### “floor” vs “profit”

You likely meant “floor,” but it sounded closer to “profit.”

Practice:

- “higher floor”
- “lower floor”

---

## E. Repeated Weakness Patterns

- Missing articles like a/an/the
- Using direct translation from Korean
- Good follow-up question ability
- Strong conversation continuation skill

---

## F. Quiz Candidates

1.

- 질문: “조식이 포함되어 있나요?”
- Answer: “Is breakfast included?”

2.

- 질문: “조기 체크인 가능할까요?”
- Answer: “Can I check in early?”

3.

- 질문: “추가 키 하나 받을 수 있을까요?”
- Answer: “Could I get an extra key?”

4.

- 질문: “체크아웃 시간이 언제인가요?”
- Answer: “What time is check-out?”

5.

- 질문: “헬스장 이용할 수 있나요?”
- Answer: “Can I use the gym?”
